**Use for:** an event of a Google calendar. It is the core event's title, times, place and recurrence, with Google's own facts beside them. Changes to an event made in Marfa are carried back to Google.

**Recurrence:** a series is one row carrying its rule. A changed occurrence is its own row, a child of the series through `parent-of`.

**Edges:** `google.calendar.in-calendar` runs to its calendar.
